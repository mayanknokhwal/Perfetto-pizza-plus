Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • Perfetto Pizza, Raisinghnagar, Rajasthan

1. Order Cancellation Policy (Strict 1-Minute Rule)

At Perfetto Pizza, we prioritize ultra-fast, fresh food preparation. As soon as you confirm an order, our kitchen chefs immediately begin stretching fresh dough, applying gourmet sauces, and baking your meals.

⏳ Strict 1-Minute Window: Customers may request order cancellation ONLY within 1 minute of placing the order, provided our kitchen has not yet accepted or started food preparation.

Once the order status moves to "Preparing", "Accepted", or "Out for Delivery", cancellations are strictly prohibited because customized fresh ingredients cannot be reused.

2. Perishable Food Items Notice (Non-Returnable Goods)

In compliance with government food safety regulations (FSSAI) and the inherently perishable nature of hot culinary goods:

  • No Physical Returns: Once a food item, pizza, burger, pasta, wrap, or beverage is delivered to the customer, it is classified as Non-Returnable.
  • We do not accept physical returns or item exchanges once the package seal has been delivered.

3. Eligibility Criteria for Refunds

You are entitled to a full or partial refund under the following verified circumstances:

  • Failed / Duplicate Transactions: Your bank account or UPI was debited, but the transaction timed out and no order was generated in our system.
  • Store Cancellation: The order was cancelled by Perfetto Pizza due to kitchen equipment maintenance, raw material unavailability, or unexpected store closure.
  • Undeliverable Location: Our delivery fleet could not fulfill the order due to operational or accessibility roadblocks in Raisinghnagar.
  • Verified Defect / Wrong Item: In the rare event of severe food damage, completely missing items, or wrong food delivery verified by our customer care desk within 30 minutes of delivery.

4. Refund Processing Timelines & Modes

All approved refunds are initiated through our authorized payment partner (PhonePe):

⏱️ 5 to 7 Business Days: Once approved, refunds are credited automatically back to the original source account (UPI, Bank Account, Debit/Credit Card, or Wallet) within 5 to 7 banking days.

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders with verified store-side issues, approved refunds are transferred via direct UPI transfer upon customer verification.

5. How to Report an Issue or Request a Refund

If you experience any issue with your order, please contact our support team immediately within 30 minutes of delivery:
