Data Protection & Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • Perfetto Pizza, Raisinghnagar, Rajasthan

1. Introduction & Overview

At Perfetto Pizza ("we", "our", "us"), protecting the privacy and personal data of our customers is a foundational priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you use our online food ordering platform in Raisinghnagar, Rajasthan.

2. Information We Collect (Strictly for Order Fulfillment)

We collect only the essential personal details necessary to prepare your fresh meals and deliver them accurately to your doorstep:

  • Customer Name: To address and identify your order packages correctly.
  • Mobile Phone Number: To send order confirmation alerts, OTP verifications, and enable our delivery driver to reach you at your doorstep.
  • Email Address: For dispatching one-time security login codes, electronic order receipts, and cross-device account profile recovery.
  • Hyperlocal Delivery Address: Colony/House details, street name, nearby landmark, ward number, and GPS pin coordinates strictly utilized to calculate delivery distance zones and navigate our delivery drivers.
  • Order Items & Cart History: To facilitate live kitchen tracking and re-ordering convenience.

3. Secure Payment Handling (PhonePe / RBI Gateways)

We believe in zero storage of sensitive financial credentials:

Zero Card / Financial Storage: Perfetto Pizza does NOT collect, view, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, CVV codes, bank passwords, or UPI PINs.

All digital transactions are processed securely through PhonePe and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment aggregators with 256-bit SSL encryption and strict PCI-DSS compliance.

4. Zero Third-Party Data Selling or Sharing

We enforce a strict NO SPAM, NO DATA SELLING policy:

  • We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or disclose your personal contact information to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • Your delivery address and phone number are shared solely with our dedicated, verified delivery personnel for the exclusive purpose of fulfilling your active order.

5. Data Storage & Cloud Infrastructure

Customer delivery profiles and active orders are persisted on secure, enterprise-grade cloud servers (Firebase Firestore) with end-to-end HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and at rest.

Our platform uses standard browser LocalStorage solely to maintain your local shopping cart state, dark/light theme preference, and cached delivery addresses across browsing sessions.

6. Customer Rights & Profile Updates

You have complete control over your delivery profile:

  • You can edit or update your delivery address, colony, landmark, or GPS coordinates anytime directly in the Profile tab.
  • You can clear your local order history using the "Clear History" button on the app.
  • To request complete deletion of your customer profile from our records, contact our support team.
